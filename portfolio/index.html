About Me

You can call me Rane — a self-taught developer from Myanmar with hands-on experience in both web development and embedded/IoT systems. Since 2021, I have enjoyed turning curiosity into practical software and hardware projects.

On the web side, I build applications with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Tailwind CSS, and Python/Django. In hardware and IoT, I work with ESP32 and Arduino boards, sensors, motor control, and MQTT cloud integration.

I built MakerHub MM to help young learners in Myanmar explore coding and electronics, and I am currently rebuilding it in React with Tailwind CSS. Alongside my current projects, I am expanding my learning into Node.js, Next.js, DevOps basics, real-workplace collaboration workflows, and agentic coding.
